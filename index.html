03 · Industries We Serve

Industries We Serve

Oil & Gas Industry

Auras Group delivers specialized solutions for the oil and gas sector, supporting upstream, midstream, and downstream operations. We provide custom-engineered skid systems, including metering, chemical injection, and pig launching solutions, designed to withstand harsh environments. Our comprehensive range of products includes high-performance valves, piping, instrumentation, and process equipment, all meeting stringent industry standards. With a focus on efficiency and safety, we help clients optimize their operations while ensuring compliance with global regulations.

Petrochemical Industry

In the petrochemical sector, we offer robust solutions that enhance production efficiency and operational safety. Our product portfolio includes corrosion-resistant piping systems, high-pressure valves, and specialized skids such as chemical injection and nitrogen units. We understand the critical nature of petrochemical processes and provide reliable components that ensure seamless operations. By leveraging our engineering expertise and procurement capabilities, we support petrochemical plants in maintaining peak performance and minimizing downtime.

Power Generation Industry

Auras Group plays a vital role in the power generation industry by supplying essential components that enhance energy production and infrastructure reliability. Our offerings include turbine valves, heat tracing systems, and electrical instrumentation such as PLCs and junction boxes. Whether for thermal power plants or renewable energy projects, we deliver durable and efficient solutions that meet the sector’s evolving demands. Our commitment to quality ensures that power generation facilities operate at maximum efficiency while adhering to safety and environmental standards.

Water & Wastewater Treatment Industry

We provide sustainable solutions for the water and wastewater treatment industry, addressing challenges in desalination, filtration, and industrial water management. Our expertise includes designing and supplying treatment skids, sampling systems, and chemical dosing units. Additionally, we offer a wide range of pumps, sensors, and control panels to support efficient water treatment processes. By combining innovative engineering with high-quality components, we help clients achieve reliable and environmentally friendly water management solutions.

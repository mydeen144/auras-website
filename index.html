M — 01

Our Mission

To provide leadership in the engineering and manufacturing of environmental products for oil & gas, petrochemical, power and water desalination. To ensure the safety of every employee and client, deliver the highest quality products and services, and maintain a healthy and safe environment for all, every day.

V — 02

Our Vision

To be a global leader in delivering sustainable energy solutions that power progress and protect the planet for future generations, and to set the benchmark in operational excellence, safety and reliability across the oil, gas and petrochemical sectors.

02Industries

Industries We Serve

  • 01 Oil & Gas

    Custom skid systems for metering, chemical injection and pig launching, plus high-performance valves, piping and instrumentation for upstream, midstream and downstream operations.

  • 02 Petrochemical

    Corrosion-resistant piping systems, high-pressure valves and specialised skids such as chemical injection and nitrogen units that keep plants running with minimal downtime.

  • 03 Power Generation

    Turbine valves, heat tracing systems and electrical instrumentation including PLCs and junction boxes, for thermal power plants and renewable energy projects.

  • 04 Water & Wastewater

    Treatment skids, sampling systems and chemical dosing units, with pumps, sensors and control panels for desalination, filtration and industrial water management.

04Expertise

Engineering Depth at Every Stage

A highly skilled team proficient in international design codes, backed by world-class software and a fully equipped fabrication facility for turnkey project execution.

  • Design and detailed engineering to ASME VIII, B31.3, API and TEMA, using PV Elite, TEKLA and AutoCAD in-house, with ANSYS, CAESAR II and HYSYS through partners.

    Engineering capabilities

  • Skids, vessels, piping and structural modules in carbon steel, stainless, duplex and special alloys. Certified welders, full material traceability, FAT, pressure testing and third-party inspection.

    Fabrication & testing

  • Site installation of equipment, skids, piping and modules to approved drawings, then step-by-step commissioning, safety checks and a commissioning report for client approval.

    Installation & commissioning

  • Inspection, repair and performance testing to restore equipment, preventive maintenance to avoid breakdowns, and quick-response on-site support with full maintenance records.
